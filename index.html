Help

How does the app choose a method? It looks at each verse's length and rhythm, then picks chunking, chaining, fading words or memory hooks to suit it.

Voice recitation doesn't work. Allow microphone and speech recognition for Scripture Recall in your device Settings. You can always type instead.

Moving to a new phone? Settings → Your data → Export backup, then Restore on the new phone. Device backups (iCloud or Google) also include your progress.

Reminders aren't arriving. Allow notifications for Scripture Recall in your device Settings, then turn the daily reminder off and on again.
