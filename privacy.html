Total Recall: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

The short version

Total Recall has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your verses, progress, scores, notes and settings are stored only on your device.

Information stored on your device

Your memory list, collections, practice history, scores and settings are kept in a database on your phone. They are included in your device's own backups (iCloud or Google) if you have those turned on. You can export or delete them at any time in Settings → Your data.

Microphone and speech recognition

When you choose to recite a verse aloud, the app uses your microphone and your device's speech recognition service (Apple Speech on iOS, Google speech services on Android) to turn your voice into text, which is compared with the verse on your device. Depending on your device, the speech service may process audio on Apple's or Google's servers under their privacy policies. Total Recall does not record, store or send your audio anywhere else. Typing instead of speaking is always available.

Notifications

Daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No push-notification service or device token is used.

AI collection builder (optional)

In versions where cloud AI is enabled, when you ask the app to build a collection the text you type (for example “verses about Gideon”) is sent to our server and processed by Anthropic's Claude to suggest verses. Your verses, progress and personal details are never sent. Requests are not used to identify you and are not sold or shared for advertising. Without cloud AI, collections are built entirely on your device.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Scripture text

The King James Version text is in the public domain.

Changes and contact
